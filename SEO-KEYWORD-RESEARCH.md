# BY12 keyword research and implementation — 16 November 2026

This release targets relevant perfume shopping, BY12 product names, inspiration references, samples and buying decisions. It follows 23 public search queries, competitor-page inspection and a read of BY12's actual public catalog fields. The search sample is not a Pakistan-localized Google rank report. Search volume, keyword difficulty and current BY12 positions were not obtained and are not invented.

## Findings and decisions

1. Broad `perfume` results include large international fragrance retailers; Pakistan shopping searches surface established local stores and large collections. General perfume terms are a long-term discovery target. Product-specific relevance should also be strengthened rather than using the same broad title everywhere.
2. Coffeeno/Ravla queries in this sample returned unrelated coffee apps, food brands and businesses. BY12 was not observed in the returned sample. That is a discovery observation, not proof of exclusion from Google's index. The implementation makes the relationship between each name, perfume, BY12 and its actual inspiration clearer.
3. Searches for Khamrah Qahwa and Gucci Flora include both original products and inspired alternatives. BY12's titles and visible copy retain the inspired-product distinction. No claim to sell an original Lattafa/Gucci bottle or a particular unconfirmed Flora edition was introduced.
4. Sample intent includes small vials and full-size retail testers. BY12's page now explicitly describes its samples, actual product sizes, availability and delivery charges. Current stock is never inferred from a full bottle's availability.
5. Coffeeno's old search description was a truncated first-person story; Ravla's also ended mid-sentence. All four product/sample records receive complete descriptive metadata and clear catalog copy. Existing IDs and product names are retained.
6. The winter article promised ten perfumes but described ten scent categories. It is rewritten as a practical buying guide with actual Coffeeno/Ravla examples, samples, size guidance and order terms; its existing URL is preserved.

These implementation choices are based on relevance and business fit, not a claim that a keyword has low competition or a particular monthly volume.

## Content and visible changes

- Homepage: descriptive inspired-perfume title/social descriptions and a new compact “Find your BY12 perfume” section with named product, collection, tester and comparison links.
- Shop/Men/Women: shopping/category titles, clearer main headings and category-specific buying text with real catalog links.
- Testers: Pakistan sample-focused metadata, heading and introductory text, plus a comparison link. Actual sizes/prices/stock still come from the catalog.
- Coffeeno/Ravla and their 3ml testers: complete metadata, concise product descriptions and descriptive image alt fields. Scent language is paraphrased from the existing BY12 catalog, not copied from competitors or inferred from an original perfume's formula. No structured note pyramid, ingredient list, similarity percentage or wear-time guarantee was invented.
- New comparison guide: current descriptions, per-size prices, price per ml, stock and matching testers. Article/breadcrumb markup, guide-hub links and sitemap inclusion use the existing guide infrastructure.
- Winter article: corrected title, excerpt, section headings and helpful product/guide links. The article body contains no additional H1; the template supplies its main heading. Publication identity, image and author are preserved; modification time reflects the actual edit.
- Old inherited keyword lists and inconsistent authentic-luxury social copy are removed from the changed templates. Keywords are used in useful visible text, descriptive titles and crawlable links. Google's [Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials) supports that approach; [title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) cautions against repetitive, stuffed titles. A meta-keywords list is not a Google ranking factor ([supported meta tags](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags)).

## Validation and release

- The 47-test suite passed, including new safeguards for preview-only behavior, prohibited catalog fields, concurrent content edits, write preconditions and rollback.

## Outstanding work and measurement

No SEO service can guarantee a number-one result for every query; Google's [third-party SEO guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/third-party-seo) also explains the limits of ranking promises. This release improves relevance, crawlable content and purchase information. It does not prove that Google has recrawled or changed rankings.

The existing account-side Men/Women request confirmations and final GA4 Realtime count comparison remain unverified after browser control failed. The submitted sitemap exposes the collections and, after this release, the new comparison guide. PageSpeed mobile/desktop measurement previously returned HTTP 429; no field performance pass is claimed.

Next measurement requires real Search Console query/page data: Pakistan country filter, branded versus non-brand segments, impressions, clicks, CTR and average position over comparable date ranges. Pair that with organic accepted orders and fulfilled revenue, keeping COD orders separate from cash collection. Current service-account access does not supply these reports; no owner permissions or credentials were changed.

Off-page work still needs a backlink baseline, relevant editorial opportunities and verified listing eligibility. Candidate categories include Pakistan fragrance directories/reviewers, product sampling reviewers and relevant lifestyle publishers, but no external profile or submitted review is fabricated. No paid links, mass listings, unsolicited outreach or ranking guarantee was purchased. The remaining application/DIY/long-lasting CMS articles also need their own factual editorial review rather than silently inheriting a completed status from the winter rewrite.
